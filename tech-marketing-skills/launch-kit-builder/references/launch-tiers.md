# Launch tier scoring

Score each factor 0–2, total out of 10.

| Factor | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Customer impact** | Fixes or polish | Improves an existing workflow | Enables something new |
| **Audience reach** | A few customers or one segment | A major segment | Most customers or a new market |
| **Competitive significance** | Parity or below | Closes a known gap in deals | Clear differentiation competitors lack |
| **Revenue tie** | None | Supports retention/expansion | New SKU, pricing change, or pipeline driver |
| **Story value** | Hard to explain why it matters | Useful to customers | Newsworthy beyond existing customers |

- **8–10: Tier 1.** Full launch. Coordinated across product marketing, comms, sales, and customer success. Usually 4–8 weeks of lead time.
- **4–7: Tier 2.** Targeted launch. Blog, email, enablement. 2–3 weeks of lead time.
- **0–3: Tier 3.** Release notes, newsletter mention, a social post.

Adjustments:

- A feature that removes a frequent sales objection can jump a tier even if its raw score is low.
- Security fixes and breaking changes need customer communication regardless of tier — that's a support and trust issue, not a marketing one.
- Beta and preview launches usually drop one tier from GA, unless the beta itself is the news.
