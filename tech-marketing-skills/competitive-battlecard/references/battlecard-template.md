# Battlecard template

## [Our product] vs. [Competitor]

**Last updated:** date · **Owner:** name · **Next review:** date

### At a glance

- **Who they are:** one sentence (company, category, size/stage if relevant)
- **How they position themselves:** one sentence, in their framing `[Source]`
- **Who they win with:** the customer profile where they're strongest
- **Our angle in one line:** how to frame the deal

### Where we win

3–4 bullets. Each: the advantage, why it matters to the buyer, the proof.

### Where they win (and what to do about it)

2–3 bullets. Each: their advantage, then the reframe or the honest qualifier ("If the buyer only needs X, this may not be a fit").

### Landmine questions

3–5 discovery questions.

### Objection handling

| They say | What's going on | Respond with |
|---|---|---|

3–5 rows. Include "They're cheaper" if pricing is a factor.

### Proof points

Customer wins against this competitor, switch stories, relevant benchmarks. Placeholders if not provided: `[NEEDS: competitive win story]`.

### Pricing and packaging notes

How they charge, where it gets expensive, what's gated behind higher tiers. Every line sourced and dated.

### Recent moves

Last 6–12 months: releases, acquisitions, leadership changes, funding. Sourced and dated.

### Appendix: capability comparison

| Capability | Us | Them | Source / date |
|---|---|---|---|

Use: ✓ full, ◐ partial, ✗ none, ? unverified.
