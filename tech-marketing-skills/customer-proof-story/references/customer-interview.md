# Customer interview questions

Aim for 30 minutes. Interview the hands-on champion, and if possible, get one or two lines from their manager for the business outcome.

## Before

- What does your team do, and how big is it?
- What were you using for [problem] before? What wasn't working?
- What did that cost you — in time, incidents, headcount, or frustration?

## Trigger and decision

- What made you start looking when you did?
- What else did you evaluate? (Including building it yourselves.)
- What made you choose [product]? What almost made you choose something else?

## Implementation

- How long did it take to get running? Who was involved?
- What was easier than you expected? Harder?

## Results

- What's different now? Can you give me an example from last week?
- Do you track any numbers on this? (Deploy frequency, time to remediate, tickets, cost, hours.) Before and after?
- What would happen if you turned it off tomorrow?

## Close

- What would you tell a peer at another company considering this?
- What's next for how you'll use it?

Ask for numbers gently. If they don't have them, ask for estimates and label them as estimates in the story.
