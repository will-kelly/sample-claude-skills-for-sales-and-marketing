# Story formats

## Full case study (600–1,000 words)

- **Headline:** the customer outcome, with the customer name if approved. "How [Customer] cut release time from two weeks to two days."
- **At a glance box:** industry, company size, use case, products used, 2–3 key results
- **The challenge:** 1–3 paragraphs, in the customer's terms
- **Why [product]:** the evaluation and decision, including alternatives considered
- **Implementation:** how it went, realistically
- **Results:** the metrics ledger in prose, with a strong quote
- **What's next**

## Short proof snippet (75–100 words)

Customer, problem, outcome, one quote. For web pages, decks, and proposals.

## Pull quotes

3–5 quotes, each under 40 words, each able to stand alone. Attribute with name, title, company (or the approved anonymous description).
