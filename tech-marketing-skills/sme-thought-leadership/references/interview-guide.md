# SME interview guide

A good interview for thought leadership pulls out opinions and stories. Definitions and feature descriptions are already on the website.

## Before the interview

Send the expert 3–4 of the questions ahead of time, not all of them. They'll think about the big ones; the rest keeps the conversation natural. Record with permission.

## Question bank

Pick 10–12, tailored to the topic.

### Point of view

- What does most of the industry get wrong about [topic]?
- What's a piece of conventional wisdom about [topic] you've stopped believing?
- If you could change one thing about how teams approach [topic], what would it be?

### Stories

- Tell me about a time [topic] went badly. What happened, and what did you learn?
- What was the moment you realized [approach] didn't work at your scale?
- What's a decision you'd make differently if you started over?

### Specifics

- Walk me through how your team actually does [process] today, step by step.
- What numbers do you watch to know whether [thing] is working?
- What tools did you try and drop? Why?

### Forward-looking

- What will be different about [topic] in two years?
- What should a team starting on [topic] today do first?

### Follow-ups to use constantly

- "Can you give me an example?"
- "What did that look like in practice?"
- "Why do you think people disagree with you on that?"

## After the interview

Note the three strongest moments with timestamps. Those usually become the opening, the central argument, and the close.
