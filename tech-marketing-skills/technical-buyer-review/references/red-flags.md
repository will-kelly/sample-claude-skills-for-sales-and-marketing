# Red flags in tech marketing copy

These mirror the lists in `scripts/scan_copy.py`. Context matters: a flagged word isn't automatically wrong, but each one should earn its place.

## Buzzwords

seamless, seamlessly, robust, cutting-edge, next-generation, next-gen, revolutionary, revolutionize, best-in-class, world-class, industry-leading, leverage, unlock, empower, game-changing, game changer, single pane of glass, synergy, holistic, turnkey, frictionless, supercharge, transform (when vague), innovative, state-of-the-art, enterprise-grade (without specifics), mission-critical (without specifics), AI-powered (without saying what the AI does)

## Absolute claims

100%, zero (as in "zero risk," "zero downtime"), never, always, guaranteed, unhackable, bulletproof, eliminates (without scope), any, every (when overreaching)

## Unattributed numbers

Percentages or multipliers with no source on the same line: "3x faster," "reduce costs by 40%." Every one needs a "compared to what, measured how" answer.

## AI-writing tells

delve, in today's fast-paced, in the ever-evolving, it's not just X — it's Y, a testament to, navigate the complexities, the world of, at the end of the day, look no further, rest assured, elevate, tapestry, landscape (as filler), harness the power, "Whether you're A or B..." openers

## Structural problems the scanner can't catch

- Headline describes the company, not the customer's problem
- Features listed with no outcome attached
- No call to action, or five competing ones
- Pricing hidden behind "contact us" for a product aimed at developers
- No docs, demo, or trial link on a page aimed at practitioners
