# Scoring rubric

| Dimension | 1 | 3 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Clarity** | Can't tell what the product does after the first paragraph | Clear after some effort | What it does and for whom is obvious in the headline and subhead |
| **Specificity** | All adjectives and abstractions | Some concrete details mixed with generalities | Names integrations, standards, numbers, workflows; shows how it works |
| **Credibility** | Superlatives and absolutes, no proof | Some claims supported | Every significant claim backed by a source, number, customer, or demo; limits stated honestly |
| **Audience fit** | Written for no one in particular, or for the wrong persona | Right audience, wrong concerns | Speaks to what this persona is measured on, in their vocabulary |
| **Voice** | Reads as generic or machine-written | Mostly natural with some stock phrases | Sounds like a knowledgeable person talking; no filler |

**Verdict bands (average):**

- 4.5–5: Ship it
- 3.5–4.4: Ship after fixes
- 2.5–3.4: Needs a real revision
- Below 2.5: Rethink the message before editing the words
