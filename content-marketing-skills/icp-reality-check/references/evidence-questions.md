# Evidence questions

Questions that replace guesses with evidence. Adapt the wording to the market. Grouped by who to ask.

## Ask sales

- What was the event that made the last three closed deals start looking? Be specific: what happened that week?
- Who killed the last deal we lost, and what did they say?
- Which title signs, and which title actually uses the product? Are they the same person?
- What do prospects compare us to, including "build it ourselves" and "do nothing"?

## Ask support or customer success

- What do new customers misunderstand most in their first month?
- What do customers try to use the product for that it wasn't built for?
- Which customers churned, and what did they say on the way out?

## Ask a current customer

- What were you doing about this problem before you bought anything?
- Who else had to agree before you could buy? What did each of them care about?
- What almost stopped you?
- Where do you go when you need to learn something about this area? Name the actual sources.
- How would you describe what we do to a peer, in your own words?

## Ask a lost prospect (if you can get the call)

- What did you choose instead, and why?
- What did we say that didn't land?
- What would have had to be true for you to pick us?

## Ask internally, before any of the above

- When did anyone on the marketing team last talk to a customer directly?
- How many conversations went into this ICP? Can we see the notes?
