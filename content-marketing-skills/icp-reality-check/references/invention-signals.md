# Invention signals

Lines that suggest the ICP was written from a desk, not from customer contact.

## Pains

**Analyst-report pains.** "Struggling with digital transformation." "Facing increasing pressure to do more with less." True of everyone, so useless.
*What contact sounds like:* "Our audit found 40 service accounts nobody owns, and I have to explain that to the board next month."

**Pains that conveniently match the product.** Every listed pain is something the product solves. Real buyers have pains you can't fix, and knowing them shapes positioning.

**No ranking.** Eight pains listed with equal weight. A real buyer has one that keeps them up and seven they'd get to eventually.

## Role and motivation

**Title soup.** "VP of Engineering, Director of Platform, Head of DevOps, CTO." Four very different jobs collapsed into one persona.

**Aspirational motivations.** "Wants to be seen as an innovator." "Passionate about driving change." Nobody describes themselves this way to a vendor.

**Missing boss.** No mention of who the buyer reports to or what that person is measuring them on.

## Triggers

**No triggers at all.** The ICP describes who the buyer is but never what makes them start looking. Triggers drive timing, and timing drives content.

**Generic triggers.** "When they're ready to scale." What event? A failed audit, a new CISO, an outage, a funding round, a mandate?

## Objections

**Strawman objections.** "Concerned about price." "Worried about change." Objections the product team already has an easy answer for.
*What contact sounds like:* "We tried a tool like this two years ago and it took six months to tune out the false positives."

**No competitor named.** Real buyers compare to something, including doing nothing or building it themselves.

## Buying process

**Single-buyer assumption.** One persona makes the decision alone. In B2B tech, almost never true.

**Missing procurement and security.** No mention of security review, legal, procurement, or a budget cycle.

## Channels

**Channel wishlist.** "Active on LinkedIn, reads industry publications, attends conferences." Every B2B persona says this.
*What contact sounds like:* Named newsletters, named Slack communities, named podcasts, "asks their team in a standup."

## Language

**Buyer speaks in your marketing copy.** The persona's pains use your product's taglines. Customers don't talk that way.

**No direct quotes.** An ICP built from conversations usually has at least a few quotes. None at all is a signal.
