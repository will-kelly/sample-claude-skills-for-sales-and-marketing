# Example: ICP for a cloud cost management tool

## Input (excerpt)

> **Primary persona: Cloud-forward technology leader**
> Title: VP of Engineering, Director of Cloud, Head of FinOps, or CTO
> Pains: Struggling with rising cloud costs. Lacks visibility into spend. Under pressure to do more with less. Wants to drive a culture of cost accountability.
> Motivations: Passionate about innovation and efficiency. Wants to be seen as a strategic partner to the business.
> Objections: Price. Concern about implementation effort.
> Channels: LinkedIn, industry publications, conferences, peer networks.

**Provenance:** Written by product marketing from a positioning workshop. Unknown number of customer conversations; not reviewed by sales.

## Section confidence

| Section | Rating | Quoted line | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Role | Invented | "VP of Engineering, Director of Cloud, Head of FinOps, or CTO" | Four jobs with different bosses and metrics. A FinOps lead and a VP of Engineering want opposite things from a cost tool. |
| Pains | Plausible | "Struggling with rising cloud costs" | Probably true, but true of every cloud customer. No ranking, no specifics. |
| Motivations | Invented | "Passionate about innovation and efficiency" | Aspirational. Nobody describes themselves this way to a vendor. |
| Triggers | Missing | — | No idea what makes them start looking. |
| Objections | Invented | "Price. Concern about implementation effort." | Strawmen with stock answers. No competitor, no past failure. |
| Channels | Invented | "LinkedIn, industry publications, conferences, peer networks" | Channel wishlist. Nothing named. |

## Questions to replace guesses

**Sales:** What happened the week the last three deals started? A surprise bill, a CFO mandate, a new FinOps hire? Who signed, and who uses it?

**Customer success:** Which persona actually logs in weekly? What do they misunderstand in month one?

**A current customer:** What were you using before (spreadsheets, native cloud tools, a competitor)? Who else had to agree? Where do you go to learn about cloud cost? Name the newsletter or community.

## Content risk

Content built on this ICP will try to talk to a VP of Engineering and a FinOps analyst in the same paragraph and land with neither. With no triggers, nothing will be timed to the moment a buyer starts looking, so it'll read as evergreen thought leadership competing with every other vendor's.

## Fix first: triggers

Get the buying trigger from three recent deals. It tells you which persona really starts the search, and that decides who every other section should describe.
