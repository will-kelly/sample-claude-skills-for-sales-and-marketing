# Theater patterns

Match these against the copy. Quote each matching line.

## Absolutes that can't be true

**Eliminate / zero / never.** "Eliminate vulnerabilities." "Zero downtime." "Never miss a threat." Every practitioner knows the exception, and once they think of it they stop reading.
*Fix:* Scope the claim to what the product actually controls.

**Complete / total / full visibility.** Nobody has full visibility. Readers know it's false and assume the rest is too.
*Fix:* Name what's covered and, ideally, what isn't.

## Magic without mechanism

**AI-powered with no verb.** "AI-powered security." What does the model do? Classify? Prioritize? Summarize? Generate fixes?
*Fix:* Name the task the model performs and what a human still does.

**"Automatically" doing hard things.** "Automatically remediates misconfigurations." Practitioners hear "changes production without asking."
*Fix:* Say what's automatic, what needs approval, and how to roll back.

**One-click.** One click to do what, with what permissions, after what setup?
*Fix:* Be honest about setup and keep the "one click" for the step that really is one click.

## Category confusion

**Shift left as a destination.** "Shift left with [Product]." Shift left is a practice, and the hard part is developer adoption, not the tool.
*Fix:* Say where in the pipeline it runs and what a developer sees.

**Zero trust in a box.** Zero trust is an architecture and a program. A product can support it; it can't be it.
*Fix:* Name the capability (identity-aware access, microsegmentation, continuous verification of X).

**Platform when it's a feature.** If it does one thing well, saying "platform" invites the comparison with the vendors who really are platforms.
*Fix:* Own the one thing.

## Proof that isn't proof

**Unsourced percentages.** "Reduce MTTR by 70%." Compared with what, measured how, on whose data?
*Fix:* Source and method, or `[NEED: benchmark with method]`.

**Analyst name-drops without context.** "Recognized by [firm]" with no report name, date or category.
*Fix:* Cite the specific report and placement, or cut it.

**Logo walls for a technical claim.** Logos prove someone bought it, not that it works the way the copy says.
*Fix:* One customer quote from an engineer that names the specific problem.

## Tells that the writer never used it

**Wrong layer.** Describing a runtime security tool as a scanner, or a scanner as runtime protection.
**Wrong user.** Selling to developers with CISO language ("reduce enterprise risk posture").
**Wrong pain.** Leading with a problem practitioners don't rank highly ("struggling with dashboards?") instead of the one they do.
**Buzzword stacking.** Three or more of: AI-native, cloud-native, next-gen, end-to-end, holistic, unified, seamless, in one sentence.
*Fix:* Interview one user. Seriously. Then rewrite from what they said.
