# Buyer frame map

How commercial value language maps to what federal stakeholders actually care about. If a commercial claim has no row here and no honest equivalent, cut it.

## Value translation

| Commercial frame | Federal frame | Who in the buying chain cares |
|---|---|---|
| Revenue growth | Mission outcomes delivered; backlog reduced; service levels met | Mission owner, program office |
| Competitive advantage | Capability gap closed; modernization milestone met | Program office, CIO staff |
| Speed to market | Time to authorization; time to operational capability | ISSO, authorizing official, program manager |
| Cost savings | Cost avoidance, legacy decommissioning, license consolidation | CFO staff, contracting officer |
| Customer experience | Public-facing service delivery; accessibility; trust | Mission owner, CX lead |
| Developer productivity | Workforce capacity against hiring constraints | CIO staff, program office |
| Reduced risk | Audit findings closed; POA&M items retired; inherited controls | ISSO, inspector general exposure, authorizing official |
| Innovation | Usually nothing. Only use if tied to a named modernization mandate the buyer already has. | — |

## The buying chain, briefly

Write so each of these people can find their sentence:

- **Mission owner** wants to know the mission problem gets solved
- **Program manager** wants to know it fits the schedule and the existing architecture
- **Contracting officer** wants to know how it's bought and whether the claims will survive scrutiny
- **ISSO / security** wants to know the authorization story and what controls are inherited
- **Authorizing official** wants to know the risk is understood and documented

A good federal page gives each of them one clear paragraph. A commercial page usually only talks to the first one.

## Plain-language checks

- Spell out every acronym after FedRAMP, ATO and CUI on first use
- One idea per sentence in anything a contracting officer might quote
- No metaphors about journeys, partnerships, or transformation
