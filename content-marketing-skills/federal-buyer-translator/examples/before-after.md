# Example: observability platform landing page

## Input (commercial hero section)

> **Ship faster with total visibility.**
> Trusted by 2,000+ engineering teams including Fortune 100 leaders. Our FedRAMP compliant platform gives you government-grade security and cuts incident costs by 40%. Start your free trial in minutes.

## Claims audit

| Line | Category | Why a federal reviewer stops | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| "Trusted by 2,000+ engineering teams including Fortune 100 leaders" | Commercial-only proof | Says enterprise, not mission. No public sector reference. | Replace with an approved agency reference or a placeholder. |
| "FedRAMP compliant platform" | Compliance overstatement | No impact level, no stage. "Compliant" isn't a FedRAMP status. | `[FedRAMP status: level and stage]` |
| "government-grade security" | Compliance overstatement | Undefined. Reads as filler for a missing authorization. | Delete. |
| "cuts incident costs by 40%" | Unverifiable outcome | Commercial ROI, unsourced, unscoped. | Source and scope, or move to a linked commercial case study. |
| "Start your free trial in minutes" | Procurement mismatch | Click-through terms and free offers don't work for most federal users. | "Request a pilot" or "Talk to our federal team." |

## Federal rewrite

> **See what's failing before your mission does.**
> Agency systems now span legacy data centers, multiple clouds and contractor-run services, and outages surface first as delayed public services. [Product] gives operations teams one view across all of it, so incidents get found and fixed before they reach the public.
>
> [FedRAMP status: confirm impact level and stage]. [If authorized: Agencies can leverage the existing package and inherit [N] controls, which shortens the path to their own ATO.]
>
> [NEED: approved federal reference or public sector case study]
>
> Available through [contract vehicle, only if true]. **Talk to our federal team about a pilot.**

## Open questions before this ships

1. What is the exact FedRAMP impact level and stage, and is the package in the marketplace?
2. Which contract vehicles is the product actually on?
3. Do you have an agency willing to be named, or a public sector case study legal has cleared?
4. Where did the 40% figure come from, and does it apply to anything resembling a government environment?

## What not to say on a sales call

- Don't call the platform "FedRAMP compliant." Say the level and stage, or say you'll confirm.
- Don't quote the 40% figure to a contracting officer.
- Don't offer a free trial to a federal user. Offer a scoped pilot through the right channel.
