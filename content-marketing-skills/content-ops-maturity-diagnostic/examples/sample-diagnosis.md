# Example diagnosis: B2B SaaS marketing team, 4 writers

*Condensed from a fictional intake interview.*

## Maturity snapshot

| Dimension | Level | Evidence | Read |
|---|---|---|---|
| Strategy and audience | 3 | "We have three personas from a workshop last year." Sales filters requests. | Workable, but nobody's re-checked the personas. |
| Governance and review | 2 | "The last whitepaper sat with product for 19 days." Five approvers on blog posts. | This is where work goes to wait. |
| Production workflow | 3 | Briefs required; tracker in Asana, "mostly current." | Fine for now. |
| Findability and reuse | 2 | "I'd probably ask in Slack." Three versions of the same solution brief live in Drive. | People rebuild what already exists. |
| Tooling and AI use | 3 | Shared prompt library; AI drafts first passes on blog posts. | Drafts are faster. Review isn't. |
| Measurement and learning | 2 | Traffic dashboard; "we've never cut a content type." | Can't tell what's working. |

## The bottleneck: review and approval

Drafts aren't slow. Approvals are. The team cut first-draft time with AI this year, and median time from draft to publish went *up*, because more drafts now queue behind the same five reviewers. A blog post with five approvers and no turnaround expectation is a governance problem, not a capacity problem. Right now it costs roughly two to three weeks per asset and a backlog of finished drafts nobody can ship.

## Next two moves

1. **Tier review by risk (content lead, 30 days).** Blog posts and social: one approver, 2-day turnaround. Product claims and customer stories: product plus legal, 5 days. Everything else waits for a reason.
2. **Name one owner for the asset library (ops or senior writer, 60 days).** Retire duplicates of the top 20 most-requested assets and post one canonical link per topic.

## What not to do yet

Don't hire another writer or expand AI drafting. Both push more work into the review queue that's already stuck.

## What would change the diagnosis

If the 19-day review was a one-off caused by a product launch, governance may be a 3 and findability becomes the bottleneck. Check the last ten review cycles before acting.
