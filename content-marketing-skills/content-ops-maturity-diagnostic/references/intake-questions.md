# Intake questions

Ask three or four at a time, in this order. Follow up on vague answers by asking for the last concrete example.

## Round 1: context

1. What does your team produce, and roughly how much per month?
2. Who's on the team, and who outside the team requests content?
3. What made you want to look at this now? What broke?

## Round 2: strategy and audience

4. Who is your content for? How do you know?
5. When did someone on the content team last talk to a customer or prospect directly?
6. When a request comes in that doesn't fit the plan, what happens?

## Round 3: governance and production

7. Walk me through the last piece that shipped. Who touched it, and how long did each step take?
8. What's the longest a draft has sat in review in the last quarter? Why?
9. Where does the status of work in progress live? Is it current right now?

## Round 4: findability and tooling

10. If I asked you for your best asset on [their core topic], how long would it take to find it?
11. How do you know when content is out of date? Who's responsible for fixing it?
12. How is the team using AI today? Is anyone tracking it?

## Round 5: measurement

13. How do you know a piece worked?
14. What's the last thing you stopped doing because the data said it wasn't working?

## Follow-up prompts for vague answers

- "Can you give me the last specific example?"
- "Who specifically?"
- "How long, roughly, in days?"
- "What happened the last time that went wrong?"
