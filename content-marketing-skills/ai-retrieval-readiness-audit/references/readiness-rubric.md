# Readiness rubric

Six dimensions, 0 to 4 each. Total out of 24.

## 1. Single source of truth

- **0** Multiple live versions of core answers that disagree
- **1** Duplicates common; conflicts on at least one high-traffic topic
- **2** Duplicates exist but mostly agree
- **3** Canonical pages exist for core topics; duplicates link to them
- **4** One owned, canonical source per answer; old versions retired or clearly marked

## 2. Chunkability

Retrieval works on passages, not pages. A passage pulled alone has to make sense.

- **0** Long unstructured pages; answers depend on context several screens up
- **1** Headings exist but are generic ("Overview", "Details", "More info")
- **2** Descriptive headings; some sections too long or mixing topics
- **3** Most sections answer one question under a heading that names it
- **4** Sections are self-contained: they name the product, the subject and the condition, so a passage pulled alone is still correct

## 3. Freshness signals

- **0** No dates, no versions, no owners
- **1** Dates on some pages, unclear whether created or reviewed
- **2** Last-reviewed dates on most pages; many older than a year
- **3** Review dates and owners on core content; stale content mostly flagged
- **4** Review cadence enforced; expired content hidden or marked

## 4. Machine-readable answers

- **0** Key answers live in images, PDFs-in-pages, embedded video, or tables rendered as images
- **1** Many answers in screenshots with no text equivalent
- **2** Mostly text; some critical steps only in images
- **3** Text-first; images have descriptive alt text or captions
- **4** Text-first, with structured data (tables, definition lists, FAQ blocks) where answers are tabular

## 5. Scope and condition clarity

Wrong answers often come from right content applied to the wrong case.

- **0** Plan, version, region or role conditions never stated
- **1** Conditions mentioned once at the top of a long page
- **2** Conditions stated but inconsistently
- **3** Conditions stated in the sections they apply to
- **4** Conditions explicit everywhere they matter, including "this does not apply to X"

## 6. Governance

- **0** Nobody owns content; anyone or no one can edit
- **1** Owners exist for some spaces; no review process
- **2** Owners named; review happens when someone complains
- **3** Owners and a review cadence for core content
- **4** Lifecycle rules: create, review, retire. Retirement actually happens.

## Bands

- **20–24** Ready. Connect it and monitor.
- **14–19** Usable with known risks. Fix conflicts first.
- **8–13** Expect wrong answers. Fix before launch.
- **0–7** Don't connect this to an assistant yet.
