# Fix patterns

Use these names in the fix list so owners can recognize the work.

**Pick a winner.** Two pages conflict. An owner decides which is right, updates it, and redirects or retires the other. Never "merge later."

**Mark superseded.** Old content must stay for legal or historical reasons. Add a top-of-page notice naming the current source and the date it changed, and exclude it from the assistant's index if possible.

**Name the heading.** Replace generic headings with the question the section answers. "Overview" becomes "What [feature] does and who can use it."

**Restate the subject.** Sections that say "it" or "this setting" get the product and feature named in the first sentence, so the passage survives being pulled alone.

**Split the mixed section.** One heading covering two topics becomes two headings.

**Put the condition in the section.** "Available on Enterprise plans only" moves from the page intro into every section it applies to.

**Text the screenshot.** Steps that live only in images get numbered text steps; the image stays as support.

**Date and own it.** Add last-reviewed date and an owner role to the page template, then backfill core content first.

**Retire on schedule.** Content not reviewed in N months gets flagged, then hidden. Pick N per content type.

**Align marketing to docs.** When product marketing and docs disagree on a limit, a price or a capability, docs owners and PMM settle it together and both pages update the same day.
